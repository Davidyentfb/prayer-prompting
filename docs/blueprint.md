## 1. What stands today, and what changes

The present prayerprompting.com (read live, November 11, 2026) is a single promise: share what is on your heart and an AI shapes it into a prayer to read aloud, sold as a subscription ("less than a cup of coffee each week") with unlimited crafted prayers, a saved library, and new themes monthly. Its voice is Christian by default ("from your heart to His ears").

The rebuild keeps the founding promise — *when words fail, we help you find them* — and widens everything around it:

- **Every tradition is a first-class citizen.** The visitor chooses their own tradition, or none. No tradition is the default.
- **A Brain, not a generator alone.** A curated repository of prayers, affirmations, teachings, and stories, drawn from named teachers and books, with attribution on every item.
- **Real human prayer.** A portal where a request is placed in the hands of a real person, and the visitor is told when someone has prayed for them.
- **Safety as architecture.** Verified helplines are one tap from every screen, always free, and the intake itself watches for crisis and answers with help first.
- **A charitable trust, modestly profitable by design.** Tiers are priced to sustain the work and fund free access — never to fence off prayer, human prayer, or crisis help.

| Repository | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Prayers** | Written prayers by tradition and by need (grief, healing, gratitude, guidance, protection, provision, forgiveness, courage) |
| **Affirmations** | Short spiritual affirmations and declarations — including the prosperity and universal-law stream (Neville, Reverend Ike, Florence Scovel Shinn) |

Every item in every repository carries a record: **tradition · type · theme(s) · author/source · book or corpus · licence status (public domain / licensed / original paraphrase) · language · review status**. Nothing enters the public Brain unreviewed (see §8.4).

### 3.3 Curation law

1. **Attribution or it does not ship.** Every item names its source.
2. **Public domain first**; living authors and modern translations are licensed or paraphrased, never scraped wholesale.
3. **No closed material.** Ceremonies, initiatory content, and restricted ritual knowledge are never reproduced; the Brain offers published prayer and teaching as reading.
4. **Each tradition is reviewed in its own terms** — a tradition's shelf is checked by a reader of that tradition before launch.

## 4. The intake flow — the reel's method, adapted and made safe

The referenced reel teaches a quiz funnel: a short sequence of questions, answers classified in real time into buyer archetypes, each archetype routed to a tailored page. The rebuild takes that mechanism entire — and takes its warning with it: the reel's own example (Hims & Hers) drew an FTC action over sensitive quiz data. A prayer intake collects disclosures more sensitive than health data. The flow is therefore built **privacy-first by design law (§6.3)**.

**Step 0 — Safety, before everything.** A standing first screen line: "If you may harm yourself, or you are in danger, tap here first." The helplines open instantly. This never waits for the quiz to finish.
